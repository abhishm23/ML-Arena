The business question

Which orders were discounted far more than they should have been, and how much did that cost?

Every order gets a discount, and most of them make sense — the big customer, the end-of-season line, the bundle deal. The problem is the few orders where the discount is far larger than that situation ever justifies. We learn what a normal discount looks like for each kind of order, then show you the ones that blow past it and what they cost you.
